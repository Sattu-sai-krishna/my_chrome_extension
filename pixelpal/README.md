# PixelPal — Playful Browser Pet & Page Arcade

PixelPal is a playful, 100% local Chrome Extension (Manifest V3) that turns your browser into a cozy companion and stress-relief playground.

## Features
- **Expressive Browser Pet**: Pet your buddy, feed it snacks, level up, and watch it react to how many tabs you have open.
- **Pop Bubbles on Any Page**: Launch floating pastel bubbles directly over any webpage and pop them for ★ coins.
- **Star Catcher Mini-Game**: Play a built-in HTML5 Canvas arcade game inside the popup to earn coins and beat your high score.
- **15-Second Reset**: A quick guided breathing break that recharges your pet's energy and awards +10 ★ coins.
- **Wardrobe**: Unlock and equip playful accessories (Golden Crown, Cool Shades, Lo-Fi Beats) with your earned coins.

## How to Install in Chrome
1. Unzip `pixelpal-chrome-extension.zip`.
2. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `pixelpal` folder.
